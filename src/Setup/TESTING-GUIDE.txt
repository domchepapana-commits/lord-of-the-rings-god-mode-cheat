LORD OF THE RINGS GOD MODE CHEAT 1.0  -  MANUAL TESTING GUIDE FOR THE "ALL FACTIONS" PAGE

WHAT HAS BEEN TESTED  (23 Nov 2026, real skirmishes, Men against one Easy opponent)

   tests 1-10 below passed in both games. In detail: a battalion, a hero and the "already in your
   army" check; all heroes of every faction (36 / 28); a finished building that units walk around

   faction with builders and fortress; a click while the game is paused; saving and loading;

 NOT tested: most of the list entries one by one (about a dozen kinds per game were tried),

   other starting factions than Men, other patches.
 So: the page works in a first round of tests. It is not "fully verified" - use the tests below
 for anything you rely on.

READ THIS FIRST
 * The ALL FACTIONS page was written by reading the two game programs. An entry that has not
   been tried yet may still do nothing or, at worst, end the match you are in.
 * The cheats you already use (CHEATS and SETTINGS pages) are the same as in version 3.1.0. The
   new page does nothing at all until you press one of its buttons.
 * No game file, patch, map or save is changed by the trainer. The worst a failed test can do is
   end the match you are in.
 * Test in a skirmish you do not mind losing, not in a saved game you care about
   (see "SAVED GAMES" at the end).
 * Offline skirmish only, as before.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
 * The ALL FACTIONS page does nothing until one of its buttons is pressed. If it gives you
   trouble, simply do not use it - the CHEATS page works without it.
 * The trainer writes down what it did in  %APPDATA%\LordOfTheRingsGodModeCheat\session.log  (portable copy:
   TrainerData\session.log next to the program). Lines for this page start with "ALL FACTIONS".
   If a test fails, that file and the number of the test are all that is needed to find the cause.
 * A message such as "Not available on this game version" means the trainer compared the game's
   code with what was analysed, found a difference and did nothing. That is the safety check
   working, not a crash.

HOW TO OPEN THE PAGE
 Start the trainer and a skirmish as usual, press F12 in the match, then click  ALL FACTIONS  at
 the top of the menu. Click  CHEATS  (same place) to go back.
 The page shows "Your faction: ..." once a match is running; before that its buttons are grey.

Do the tests in this order - each one builds on the one before. Tick what happened.
Run tests 1-10 once in The Rise of the Witch-king and once in The Battle for Middle-earth II.

KNOWN LIMITS  (how the games are built - not faults to report)
 * New things appear at the point the camera looks at, or at the nearest free place if buildings
   or troops already stand there. The trainer knows where things stand, but it cannot see
   cliffs, slopes or water: look at flat ground, especially for buildings.
 * Only ONE faction's powers can be on the power bar at a time (the games give every player
   exactly one "spell book"). Switching between factions takes one click and keeps what was
   unlocked.
 * The game's own "buy powers" screen and the hero buttons of a fortress always belong to the
   faction you started the match with. Other factions' powers are therefore unlocked as a whole
   tree by the trainer, and other factions' heroes are recruited from this page. A hero of
   another faction that dies cannot be revived at a fortress - recruit him again here.
 * "Change to this faction" does not turn your existing units into the other faction's units,
   and it does not tell the game that you ARE the other faction. The games tie too much to
   that (for example whether your whole army counts as Good or Evil) for it to be done safely
   without testing. What you get instead is everything the other faction has: its powers, its
   builders, buildings, units and heroes - while your own army stays exactly as it is.
 * Build limits (for example "only one of this hero") are not checked for things created here,
   except that the trainer itself never creates a hero you already have.
 * Create-a-hero characters, wall pieces, fortress add-ons and creatures that belong to a lair
   are not offered: the games never create those on their own, so they could not be made safe
   without testing.

SAVED GAMES
 * The games do not store which power bar was in use. A loaded game can therefore come up
   with another faction's power bar - even if you had switched back before saving (seen in the
   first tests). From 4.0.1 the trainer notices this and puts your own power bar back by itself
   a few seconds after the load; the page then says "This saved game came back with the ...
   power bar". Press USE THIS FACTION'S POWERS again if you want the other one.
 * Without the trainer running, a loaded game may keep the other faction's bar. Start the
   trainer and it is put right.
 * A summoned creature that was on the field when you saved may be gone after loading (seen
   once with a Balrog whose time was nearly up; not looked into further).
 * Units, heroes and buildings created with this page are ordinary game objects and are saved
   and loaded like everything else.
