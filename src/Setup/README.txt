HOW TO USE
 1. Open "Lord of the Rings God Mode Cheat" from the desktop (Windows asks for administrator rights because the
    games themselves run as administrator). You can leave it running in the tray.
 2. Start either game and begin a skirmish. The trainer finds the running game by itself and
    switches to that game's look.
 3. Press F12 in the game. The trainer menu opens on top of the match - the game keeps running and
    you are not switched to the desktop. Click a switch. Press F12 again (or HIDE) to keep playing.
    Switches can also be set before a match; they apply when it starts and in every new match.

ECONOMY AND CONSTRUCTION
 Unlimited Money        Keeps your resources at 999,999.
 Instant Army           Your units and heroes recruit instantly.
 Instant Construction   Your buildings complete instantly. Walls between wall hubs are built at once
                        too, fully visible and solid.
 Instant Upgrades       Fortress upgrades, building level-ups, research and unit upgrades finish

 Hold to Recruit        Hold the left mouse button on a unit icon and the order repeats until you

GOD MODE (three separate switches)
 Units / Heroes / Buildings   Your own units, heroes or buildings take no damage while the switch
                        is on. Enemies and allies are not affected, healing still works, and you
                        can still demolish your own buildings. Off = normal damage again at once.

ARMY AND HEROES
 Unlimited Command Points   Raises your limit to 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / no limit.
 Instant Hero Level Up      LEVEL UP ALL HEROES (also F9): every hero you have on the map goes
                            to the highest level. Nobody has to be selected.
 MY HEROES                  (button next to LEVEL UP ALL HEROES) A list of every hero you have on
                            the map, with level and health - also the ones the game's own hero
                            bar has no room for (it shows 16). Click a hero: the game selects
                            that hero, the camera goes there and the menu closes, so you can
                            give orders at once. If the game is paused, resume it and click
                            again. The list is only read while that page is open.
 Freeze Enemy AI           Enemy computer players stop: their armies break off the attack and stand
                            where they are, and nothing new is built or recruited (units already in
                            a production queue still come out, for about a minute). They no longer
                            attack your units or buildings, and while frozen they hardly defend
                            themselves either. Your own side is not changed. Switch off and they
                            carry on as before: within seconds they attack and recruit again.

WEATHER (visual only - it changes nothing in the battle)
 Clear / Rain / Snow / Fog / Storm, and RESTORE DEFAULT WEATHER to get the map's own weather back.
 The weather also returns to normal when the match ends or the trainer closes.
 All modes are verified in both games. Rain and snow fit themselves to the map: the games only
 place drops between the bottom of the world and a fixed height, so on high ground (Isengard in
 Battle for Middle-earth II) they used to fall unseen inside the terrain. The trainer now keeps
 them between the ground and the camera, also when you zoom far out with Extended Zoom.
 On maps that have snow of their own, RESTORE DEFAULT WEATHER brings exactly that snow back.

ALL FACTIONS  (new in 4.0  -  first skirmish tests passed in both games; see TESTING-GUIDE.txt)
 Click ALL FACTIONS at the top of the menu. Choose a faction on the left; its heroes, units and
 buildings are listed on the right in groups (Heroes, Infantry, Cavalry, Siege, Buildings ...).
 The search box looks through all factions at once: click it, type, Enter keeps the result, Esc
 clears it.
 Click a tile                 That hero, battalion or building is created for you where the
                              camera is looking, finished and ready. Nothing is put on top of
                              anything else: if a building or troops already stand there, the
                              new thing takes the nearest free place beside them - so you can
                              click one tile after another without moving the camera. The
                              trainer cannot see cliffs or water: look at flat ground.
                              Units cost their normal command points; nothing costs money.
 RECRUIT ALL ... HEROES       All heroes of the chosen faction, or of every faction, side by side
                              around the camera point. A hero you already have is never created
                              twice.
 USE THIS FACTION'S POWERS    Puts that faction's powers on your power bar in the game and
                              unlocks its whole power tree. Works together with Unlimited Power
                              Points and No Ability Cooldowns. One faction's powers at a time;
                              switch as often as you like.
 CHANGE TO THIS FACTION       The same, plus two of its builders (and, if ticked, its fortress)
                              at the camera. Its builders build all its buildings, and those
                              recruit all its units - with the normal build menus, placement,
                              rally points and upgrades. Your own army and buildings stay yours;
                              enemies, allies and command points are not changed.
 Back to my own faction's powers   Your own power bar again.
 Saved games                  The games do not store which power bar was in use, so a loaded game
                              can come up with another faction's bar. With the trainer running, it
                              notices this and puts your own bar back by itself within seconds.
 Summon powers                (Balrog, Rohirrim, ...) need open, flat ground away from your
                              fortress - a rule of the games themselves, not of the trainer.
 What the games do not allow (explained in TESTING-GUIDE.txt, "KNOWN LIMITS"):
  * existing units cannot be turned into another faction's units, and the trainer does not
    rewrite which faction the game thinks you are - you get the other faction's powers, builders,
    buildings, units and heroes in addition to your own;
  * the game's own "buy powers" screen and the hero buttons of a fortress stay those of the
    faction you started with; a hero of another faction that dies is recruited again from the
    trainer, not revived at a fortress;
  * create-a-hero characters, wall pieces and fortress add-ons are not offered.

GOOD TO KNOW
 * Only your own player is changed. Enemy AI keeps its normal money, limits, build times and
   cooldowns. Weather is the one thing everybody sees - and it is only a picture.
 * No game, patch, map or save file is modified. Everything happens in the running game's memory
   and is undone when you switch a cheat off or press CLOSE - with one deliberate exception:
   the limit of 600 health bars per picture stays in the running game until the game itself is
   closed. The games crash on their own with about 900 or more bars on screen, and the big army
   that the cheats gave you is still there after the trainer has closed. The limit changes
   nothing else and nothing on disk.
 * Power points above 250 that you earned in the match stay when the trainer closes.
 * A saved game keeps what the cheats had given you at that moment - your money and the raised
   command point limit are part of the save and stay raised when it is loaded, also without
   the trainer.
 * If the game is paused or minimised, a few things (Reveal, Unlock All Powers, hero levels, No
   Cooldowns, Freeze AI, weather) take effect when the match resumes.
 * Saving a skirmish: switch Freeze Enemy AI off before you save. A game saved while the enemy
   is frozen remembers that the enemy does not attack you; if you load such a save, run the
   trainer once with Freeze off and it puts the enemy back to normal by itself.
 * Other trainers (for example WeMod) changing the same things at the same time can conflict;
   use one at a time.
 * The trainer keeps a small diary of what was switched and when in
   %APPDATA%\LordOfTheRingsGodModeCheat\session.log - useful if something ever goes wrong.
 * Portable copy: LordOfTheRingsGodModeCheat-1.0-Portable.zip holds the same program without an
   installer. Unzip it anywhere and start LordOfTheRingsGodModeCheat.exe; settings and the diary are then
   kept in the TrainerData folder next to the program (because of the file portable.txt).
   Delete the folder to remove it. Windows still asks for administrator rights when it starts,
   for the reason given in SECURITY.txt.
